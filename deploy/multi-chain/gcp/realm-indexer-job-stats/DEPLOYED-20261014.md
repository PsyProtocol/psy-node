## Validation

Independent source review identified two unsafe edges in the original candidate:
Realm metadata errors could enter legacy skip handling, and a failed startup
discovery could fall back to checkpoint zero behind pruned history. The
follow-up makes these paths retry safely and adds regression coverage.

Final committed source, mounted read-only in the Bookworm builder, passed
291 Indexer tests, zero failures, three existing ignored tests. Release binary
was rebuilt after the final formatting commit; its hash was verified against
the uploaded artifact and both running `/proc/<pid>/exe` files.

Four rollout unit tests passed. Independent review also required the readiness
baseline to be sampled after the old process exits and made rollback independent
of unrelated Processor PID changes. Realm0's two ordered environment files were
discovered during preflight; the first check stopped without touching runtime.
Both files are now strictly matched, backed up and hash-protected.
