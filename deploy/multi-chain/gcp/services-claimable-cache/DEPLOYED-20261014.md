The after window does not demonstrate a latency or rejection-rate improvement.
These are uncontrolled, mixed-request observations, not a paired benchmark;
they cannot establish a regression caused by the cache. Low reuse is consistent
with conservative global invalidation; invalidation counters also include TTL
expiry, so their count alone does not prove the cause. Capacity refusals persist
and need follow-up. The new paged route had only five before requests (matching
our synthetic probe) and no after requests in these windows, so there is no
meaningful natural-traffic comparison for that route.

## Rollback

Only Services is affected. The old binary and override remain intact.

```sh
ssh gcp-psy-service 'sudo -n python3 ~/services-claimable-cache-20261014/rollout.py rollback'
```

Rollback removes only `99-z-claimable-cache-20261014.conf`, retains the previous
deadline override, restarts Services and checks the original executable/config.
Never restore an old database or Nostr cursor snapshot. The cache can alternatively
be disabled by a separately reviewed environment change and Services restart.
